# Smart Campus Companion: user manual

## For everyone
- **Sign in** with your university email, or **create an applicant account**. Only an admin can give you another role.
- **Profile > Settings**: English or Français, light or dark theme, and how many minutes before class you want a reminder.
- **Feedback**: tell the team about a problem or idea (anonymous), and answer the 10-question usability survey.

## Students and course reps
| I want to... | Go to |
|---|---|
| See my next class, hall and campus | Home (top card). Tap "Hall and campus info" for directions and a Google Maps link |
| See my week | Timetable |
| Read notices and upcoming events | Home (events bar and latest notices), "All notices" |
| Chat with my lecturer and classmates | Chat, then pick the course |
| Ask a study question | Assistant (only academic questions; check answers with your lecturers) |
| See my exams and how many days are left | Home "Next exam", or "Exams and study planner" |
| Plan my revision | Exams page > Study planner, choose hours per day |
| Find notes and past questions | "Study resources" |
| See my results | Home > Results (shown once your lecturer enters scores) |

## Lecturers
| I want to... | Go to |
|---|---|
| See my next class and where it is | Home |
| See my students | Students, open a course |
| Enter or change a student's score | Students > course > the pencil next to the student |
| Message the students of a course | Chat |
| Share notes or past questions | Home > Study resources > Add resource (a https link) |

## Admins
| I want to... | Go to |
|---|---|
| Publish a notice or event, for everyone or one department and level | Notices > Publish notice |
| Give someone a role | Users |
| Schedule or delete an exam | Home > Manage exams |
| Fill the app with sample data for testing | Home > Load demo data |
| Read feedback and the survey average | Home > Feedback and survey results |

## Applicants
Register, then use **Application** (4 steps, saved as you go) and **Admission** to follow the decision.
